<h2 id="rc4-1">1.0.0-RC4.1</h2>

- Controller Gap: space between left and right clusters. Screen pixels — scale does not move them. Default 600. Arrows step 20.
- D-pad and 1-4 stay on actions with Shagu Tweaks without opening chat first.

<h2 id="rc4">1.0.0-RC4</h2>

- Full layout: three diamond controller sets (LB, default, LT). Live page is solid, the others fade.
- Glyph size Small / Medium / Large for pad icons, including Full view.
- Unit Frames page: scale Player, Pet, Target, ToT, party, and party pets. Sits under Rings.
- XP bar uses your class color. Rested stays blue under the fill.
- Bar scale grows the whole cluster, not just the icons.
- Esc menu: ConsoleUI sits at the bottom. Shop, Options, and Quit stay put.
- AddOn list title now reads ConsoleUI by HouseLegend.
- What's new text wraps instead of clipping.
- D-pad and 1-4 cast without opening and closing chat first.

<h2 id="rc3-1">1.0.0-RC3.1</h2>

- Esc menu no longer errors with Turtle Shop / Donation Rewards.

<h2 id="rc3">1.0.0-RC3</h2>

- Welcome screen on first login.
- What's new after each update.
- Bindings rework: 1-0, M, and Escape stay yours. Only LT/LB chords are written.
- Login no longer wipes keybinds. Camera and Jump stay put.
- HUD border tint on diamonds, XP, and cast.
- XP, reputation, and cast bars restyled.
- Main bar scale no longer changes side touch bars.
- Jump and other system binds no longer stick white after LT or LB.
- Bag drop (Y) asks Yes/No before destroying an item.
- Esc menu no longer overlaps Shagu Tweaks Advanced Options.
- D-pad casts work with Shagu Tweaks without opening chat first.

<h2 id="rc2">1.0.0-RC2</h2>

- Spell Placement matches settings chrome.
- Touch bars cap at 5 slots.
