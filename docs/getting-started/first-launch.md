Stand still in a quiet area. Finish this list before you spend an hour on cosmetics.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open <code>/cui</code> → <strong>General</strong> and choose Xbox or PlayStation icons.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Mouse</strong>. Standard layout: Mouse Look off. MouseLOCK layout: Mouse Look on.</li>
  <li>Walk with the left stick. If turning with A/D feels wrong, bind A/D to Strafe Left/Right in WoW’s Key Bindings. Quick Menu and Rings support both Turn and Strafe.</li>
  <li>Test the right stick. On Standard, moving the stick parks the cursor top-left and holds right-click so the camera turns. Center the stick to use the cursor. On MouseLOCK, walk to lock, then press R3 to unlock.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Bindings</strong> and bind <strong>Toggle Radial Menu</strong> to a spare button that is not B.</li>
  <li>Open the Quick Menu, steer to a harmless panel, and press A. Press B to close.</li>
  <li>Open a bag. The virtual cursor should highlight an item. A selects, B closes, and the hint bar names the actions.</li>
  <li>Open chat. ConsoleUI Keyboard should appear. Y is space, X erase, A send, B close.</li>
  <li>If you use touch bars, unlock Mouse Look before tapping them.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Rings</strong>, put one harmless spell or item on a slice, bind that Ring, then hold, aim, and release.</li>
</ol>

## Expected result

You can walk, look, open a Blizzard panel, click something with the cursor, type a short message, and fire one Ring slice — without a protected-action popup and without losing WASD.

If a step fails, stop customizing and use [Troubleshooting]({{ '/reference/troubleshooting/' | relative_url }}).
