Rings are user-made wheels. They share the Quick Menu chrome but have no center cluster.

## Create a Ring

1. Open `/cui` → **Rings**.
2. Add or select a Ring and give it a real name.
3. Place spells or items on slices. Item slices show a count badge.
4. Bind that Ring to a hold button in Bindings or WoW Key Bindings (`ConsoleUI_RING_1` … `8`).

## Use a Ring

Hold the bind, aim with the stick or WASD, and release to cast or use the slice. Empty slices do nothing.

## Limitations

- Do not bind a Ring to the same B you use to cancel.
- Rings do not change Mouse Look.
- Turn and Strafe A/D both work.
- Spells do not show a count badge. Items sum stacks in bags `0–4`.

If the wheel opens and WASD never comes back, close with the Ring cancel bind and check [Troubleshooting]({{ '/reference/troubleshooting/' | relative_url }}).
